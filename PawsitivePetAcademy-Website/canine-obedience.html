Canine Obedience Training

Our Canine Obedience Training programme provides students with practical knowledge and skills for understanding and training dogs.

Students learn positive training techniques and how to encourage good behaviour through effective communication and responsible handling.

Course Information

  • Duration: Six Months
  • Course Fee: R1,500
  • Course Type: Six-Month Programme
  • Location: Durban

What You Will Learn

  • Understanding basic canine behaviour
  • Positive reinforcement techniques
  • Basic obedience commands
  • Responsible dog handling
  • Improving communication between owners and dogs
  • Encouraging positive behaviour

Who Is This Course For?

This programme is suitable for individuals who want to develop their knowledge of dog training and behaviour. It can also be useful for people interested in developing skills for the pet care industry.
