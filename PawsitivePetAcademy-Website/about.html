About Our Academy

Learning inspired by the pets we love.

Pawsitive Pet Academy is a Durban-based pet care education and training academy focused on helping people develop practical knowledge and skills in animal care.

Our programmes are designed for people who want to learn more about pets, develop professional skills or simply improve their understanding of responsible animal care.

Our Purpose

Our purpose is to provide accessible and practical education that encourages responsible pet care and positive relationships between people and animals.

Our Values

Care

We believe that every animal deserves responsible, respectful and compassionate care.

Connection

We encourage positive relationships between people and their pets through better understanding and communication.

Growth

We aim to help students develop knowledge and skills that can support their personal and professional goals.

How We Teach

Practical Pet Care

Our programmes focus on useful knowledge and practical approaches to caring for animals.

Positive Training

We encourage positive approaches to animal training and behaviour.

Professional Foundations

Our programmes can help students build a foundation for future opportunities within the pet care industry.
