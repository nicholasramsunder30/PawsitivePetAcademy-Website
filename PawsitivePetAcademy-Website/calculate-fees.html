A 15% VAT rate applies to the course fees.

Fee Calculation Example

The final fee is calculated by applying the applicable course discount first and then adding 15% VAT.

For example, if a student selects one R1,500 course:

  1. Course fee: R1,500
  2. 5% discount: R75
  3. Discounted fee: R1,425
  4. 15% VAT: R213.75
  5. Total: R1,638.75
