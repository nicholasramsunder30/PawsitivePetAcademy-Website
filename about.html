About our academy

Learning inspired by the pets we love.

Pawsitive Pet Academy is a Durban-based learning community focused on practical pet care education and animal training.

Our purpose

Helping people care with confidence.

We make pet education approachable for owners, aspiring groomers, trainers and pet business entrepreneurs. Our purpose is to encourage responsible care and stronger relationships between people and their animals.

Through clear guidance and practical learning, students develop useful knowledge they can apply at home or as they explore a future in pet care.
