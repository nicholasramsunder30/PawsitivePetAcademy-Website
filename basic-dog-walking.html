Basic Dog Walking

Build confidence in safe, responsible and enjoyable dog walking.

About this short course

Our Basic Dog Walking short course teaches the basics of safe and responsible dog walking, including handling and everyday walking practices.

Students learn how to choose the right equipment, manage a dog confidently on the lead and keep both dogs and people safe in public spaces.

What you will learn

  • Choosing collars, harnesses and leads
  • Safe and confident lead handling
  • Loose-lead walking techniques
  • Road safety and public spaces
  • Managing encounters with other dogs
  • Weather, hydration and exercise needs

Who is this course for?

This short course is suitable for dog owners, volunteers and anyone interested in offering a professional dog walking service.
